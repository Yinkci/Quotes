# Extra rules added to every image prompt (one per line). Lines starting with # are ignored.
# The art style itself is in style.txt; things never to draw are in avoid.txt.
This is a background for a short quote reel: each image visualizes the meaning and feeling of its line of the quote, not a literal story.
Be creative with WHAT is shown: a lone figure in a wide landscape, a couple, a close-up of hands or a face in shadow, an empty room, a road, a forest path, the sea, a window at night, a sky. Use visual metaphors that fit the words, and pick a different subject, place, time of day and color mood from the other images of this video.
Be strict about HOW it is drawn: always the exact vintage 90s anime art style of the style examples (cel line work, painted backgrounds, print grain, palette), so every image looks like it comes from the same channel.
Keep it simple and readable at a glance: one clear subject, lots of calm space around it.
The caption will be placed over the middle of the image, so keep the center area calm (sky, water, a wall, a dark shadow); put the subject below or to the side of the center.
