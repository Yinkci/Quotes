Built-in sounds.

cassette_play.wav - pressing Play on a cassette player, then tape hiss (2.4 s).
  Trimmed and level-adjusted from "Cassette Tape Play" by albertomarun on Freesound
  https://freesound.org/people/albertomarun/sounds/423875/  (Creative Commons 0 - free to use, no credit needed)

cassette_run.wav - the tape running after Play (hiss and motor), a 17 s seamless loop played under the
  whole video. From the same recording (2.5-21 s), same level as cassette_play.wav.

radio_tuning.wav - "tuning in": 1 s of real FM static with a band sweeping down through it (the dial
  turning) and a faint whistle. From "FM Radio Tuning Sweep" by davidbain on Freesound (13.6-14.6 s),
  https://freesound.org/people/davidbain/sounds/259706/  (Creative Commons 0)

radio_off.wav - the sign-off click at the end of the video: the Stop button of the same cassette
  recording as cassette_play.wav (Creative Commons 0).
